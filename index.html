Before you order

A few things to know before getting yourself into some delicious trouble.

A little about our kitchen

Feral Baking Co. is a small, home-based microbakery operating under the Arizona Cottage Food Program. Our products are made in small batches in a home kitchen, with plenty of care and a healthy amount of sourdough starter.

Home kitchen disclosure

This product was produced in a home kitchen that may come in contact with common food allergens and pet allergens and is not subject to public health inspection. Please review each product's ingredient list before ordering. Our products may contain or come into contact with common allergens. If you have a food allergy or dietary restriction, please contact us before placing an order. We cannot guarantee an allergen-free environment.

The menu

Choose from our standard menu or see what's causing trouble on our rotating specialty menu. Specialty items are offered in limited batches and may not be available every week. Press Order on any bake to choose your quantity and pay on Stripe's secure page.

How ordering works

  1. Browse the menuChoose from our standard menu or see what's causing trouble on our rotating specialty menu. Specialty items are offered in limited batches and may not be available every week.
  2. Place your preorderAll orders are preorder-only. Select your products and quantities, then complete checkout through our secure online payment system. Orders must be placed by Wednesdays at 6PM for the upcoming bake.
  3. Watch for confirmationOnce your order is submitted and payment is complete, you'll receive an order confirmation. We'll send a separate notification when your order is ready for pickup.
  4. Pick up your goodiesOrders are available for pickup on Saturdays from 9AM to noon. Your pickup instructions and location will be provided in your order confirmation or ready-for-pickup notification. Please plan to collect your order within the designated pickup window.

Pickup policies

  • Orders are made fresh to fulfill scheduled preorders.
  • Please double-check your order and pickup details before submitting payment.
  • If you need to make a change or have a question, contact us as soon as possible.
